# Style "Word-Flash" (reference: v24044gl…MP4, 73 s)

What the reference does, measured frame by frame:

- **Format:** 16:9 (1024×576 in the reference), 30 fps, about 70 s.
- **Text:** exactly ONE word on screen at a time, in the middle of the frame, in large, heavy capitals (geometric sans, about 15% of the image height).
  - A new word comes with each spoken or sung word, every ~0.2–0.5 s. Stressed words stay on longer and repeat.
  - The word is *not* a flat overlay. Its fill is cut out of the scene underneath (the background is shifted, colour-swapped and set to ~70–90% opacity), so it picks up the scene's colours. On dark scenes it is cream with a slight tint.
  - Hard swap from word to word, with no fade or pop.
- **Scenes:**
  - Flat-vector, saturated, glowing palette; one scene per thought, held 3–6 s (about 15 scenes in 70 s).
  - Inside a scene the camera always keeps drifting: a slow pan, zoom or parallax, with small loops going on (a waterfall, a blink, an insect flying, a character breathing).
  - From scene to scene: a hard cut on the beat, or a short soft wipe. No big transition effects.
- **Content:** short everyday or emotional scenes, each with one character and a clear expression (lying in bed, sick on the sofa, at the computer, sitting at night on a cliff).
- **Audio:** a single continuous track with no SFX.
- The TikTok logo at the end is the TikTok export watermark. It is **not** part of the style.

For our version: every scene comes from our own design kit (src/design/kit.tsx). No Kurzgesagt motifs and no third-party music.
