3. Description of services

Free Scrims: Taking part in regular scrim rounds is free and unlimited. Participants can win tokens through their placements. In team rounds, the prize money for a placement is split evenly between the participating team members – regardless of who paid the entry fee.

Masterclass plans: For a one-time payment, users unlock additional features for a limited period (2, 7 or 10 days depending on the deal chosen), including access to all dropmaps, a snipe allowance, full shop access and the right to pay out tokens won during the plan as balance, plus a one-time token bonus. This is a one-time purchase without automatic renewal — the extended access ends automatically when the period expires, and you can buy again at any time. If a new plan is bought while a previous plan is still running, the duration, token bonus and snipe allowance of the new purchase are added to the existing ones.

Dropmaps: Dropmaps are digital landing-spot maps. A single dropmap can be unlocked permanently in the shop for 5 tokens, a random dropmap for 2 tokens. Alternatively, a dropmap can be unlocked for 24 hours for 2 snipes; access then ends automatically. With an active Masterclass plan, all dropmaps are unlocked for the duration of the plan. Preview images are visible to everyone, the full map only to entitled users; sharing it is not permitted. Unlocking happens immediately; details on the right of withdrawal can be found in the withdrawal policy.

5. Tokens, balance and snipes

Tokens, balance and snipes are internal platform units with no independent monetary value outside ScrimPass. There are two kinds of tokens: withdrawable tokens (from the bonus of a Masterclass plan purchase or from placements in rounds with a paid entry fee) and free tokens (from placements in rounds played for free). Both kinds can be redeemed for services in the internal shop; only withdrawable tokens can also be converted manually into withdrawable balance (euros) at a 1:1 ratio. Free tokens can't be converted into balance. Paying out balance requires correct bank details on file and takes place after we have reviewed it.

Welcome offer: Each account can receive 2 welcome tokens once, as soon as two new people have registered via its personal referral link with Google or Discord and the account itself is linked to Epic Games. Welcome tokens can only be used to enter scrim rounds; they can't be redeemed in the shop or converted into balance. Winnings from rounds whose entry fee was paid with welcome tokens are frozen at first. Together with any unused welcome tokens, they become withdrawable tokens once the account has taken part in three further completed rounds whose entry fee was paid with tokens from a Masterclass plan. In case of abuse (e.g. self-created second accounts posing as "friends"), welcome tokens and resulting winnings may be withdrawn.
