Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Controller: see Legal notice

This English version is provided for convenience. Only the German version is legally binding.

1. Your rights as a data subject

You have the right at any time to:

  • request information about the personal data we process (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • request the correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • request the deletion of your data (Art. 17 GDPR), unless statutory retention obligations prevent this,
  • request the restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • receive the data you provided in a structured, commonly used format (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by an external hosting provider (Render Services, Inc., USA) on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany; its network additionally uses an upstream protection and delivery service (Cloudflare). Personal data collected on this website is processed on the host's servers. Each time the website is accessed, the host automatically records so-called server log files transmitted by your browser (IP address, date and time of the request, page accessed, referrer URL, browser type). This data is used to provide the service and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in smooth operation). To prevent abuse, we briefly count requests per IP address in memory. As Render and Cloudflare are based in the USA, access from the USA can't be ruled out; both providers base such transfers on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

3. Cookies, local storage and fonts

We use a technically necessary session cookie to recognise you as signed in. Without this cookie, sign-in doesn't work. If you follow another player's referral link, we store another technically necessary cookie ("sp_ref") for up to 30 days so that the referral can be attributed when you register. Your chosen language is stored in a cookie ("sp_lang") and in your browser's local storage so the site is shown in that language. Your settings (contrast, brightness, sounds) are stored only locally in your browser (local storage) and are not transmitted to us. No tracking or marketing cookies are used. The legal basis is Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR (performance of a contract or legitimate interest in the site working properly). The fonts used are served from our own server; no connection is made to Google's servers.

4. Registration and sign-in with Discord or Google

You sign in via "Sign in with Discord" or "Sign in with Google" (OAuth2). With Discord, Discord sends us your Discord user ID, your Discord username and your profile picture. With Google, we don't send any data of our own to Google; Google shares your Google identifier (sub ID), email address, name and profile picture with us. This data is used to create and manage your ScrimPass account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract). The privacy policies of Discord (discord.com/privacy) and Google (policies.google.com/privacy) also apply. You can disconnect or add either connection in your profile at any time.

5. Linking Epic Games (Fortnite)

You can link your Epic Games account. We then store your Epic account ID and your display name. Linking isn't needed to browse the site, but it is needed to take part in scrim rounds, as that is the only way to match your Fortnite results to your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract). An Epic account can only be linked to one ScrimPass account. You can unlink it in your profile at any time. The Epic Games privacy policy also applies: epicgames.com/site/en-US/privacypolicy.

6. User account, gameplay and results

To run the scrim rounds, we process your username, your entries, placements, token, snipe and balance transactions, team memberships and invites, and purchased dropmaps and Masterclass plans (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your username, profile picture and placements are visible in the leaderboard of completed rounds. Other signed-in users can view the participant list of an open round and your game stats (matches, win rate, round wins, token balance) by spending snipes; we store who unlocked which content. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (running the competitions, legitimate interest in a transparent, competitive platform). When you buy a Masterclass plan, we also store the time of your consent to immediate performance and to the expiry of your right of withdrawal as proof (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR).

7. Optional SP-Client and replay analysis

The SP-Client is a voluntary add-on program for Windows. It reads Fortnite's log file locally to detect the start and placement of your match, and sends us your result, a device name and an access token. Optionally, it uploads your match's replay file. From a replay file, the players' Epic account IDs and the time they were eliminated are read in order to reliably determine the placements of all participants in the round; the file itself is deleted after analysis. The analysis therefore also concerns fellow players who don't use the client. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (running the competitions) and (f) GDPR (protection against manipulated results). You can quit the client at any time and disconnect it in your profile.

8. Cheat reports

If you report a player, we store your report (name of the reported player, description and any uploaded evidence photos) and your account ID in order to review the allegation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in fair competition). Reports are only visible to us.

9. Notifications

We send you notices within ScrimPass (bell icon), e.g. about team invites, match requests, reminders before a round starts, payouts, referral rewards, results and announcements. We don't send any emails or push notifications outside the site ourselves. The exceptions are payment receipts and refund confirmations, which our payment provider Stripe sends to the email address given at checkout after a purchase (see section 11).

10. Referral programme

Every user receives a personal referral link. If a new person signs up through it, we store the link to the referring account and check whether the referred person has linked an Epic account. The referring user only sees the number of referred people, not their identity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

11. Payment processing via Stripe and payment methods

Masterclass plan purchases are processed by the payment provider Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland). When you click "Buy", you are redirected to a payment page operated by Stripe. There you enter your payment details (e.g. card details) and your email address directly with Stripe — they never pass through our own servers. We only send Stripe the chosen deal, the price, and your ScrimPass account ID and username, so that the payment can be assigned to your account. In return, we only receive confirmation from Stripe of whether and when the payment was successful.

Stripe also processes your data to prevent fraud (including device and connection data) and, after a purchase, sends a payment receipt or, in the case of a refund, a confirmation to the email address provided. Stripe is an independent controller for this processing: stripe.com/privacy. If you use Stripe's quick checkout "Link", Stripe stores your payment details for future purchases at your request.

Depending on the payment method chosen, the data required for the payment is also passed on to the respective provider, who likewise processes it under its own responsibility:

  • Credit and debit cards: your card issuer and the respective card network (e.g. Visa, Mastercard).
  • Apple Pay: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland — apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google Pay: Google Ireland Limited, Ireland — policies.google.com/privacy.
  • PayPal: PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., Luxembourg — paypal.com/legalhub/privacy-full.
  • Klarna: Klarna Bank AB (publ), Sweden. Klarna may check your identity and creditworthiness to assess the chosen payment method — klarna.com/international/privacy-policy.
  • Amazon Pay: Amazon Payments Europe S.C.A., Luxembourg — pay.amazon.com.

Which payment methods are shown to you depends on your country, device and browser. In the course of payment processing, data may also be transferred to countries outside the EU (including the USA); the providers named base this on appropriate safeguards (including EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and, for fraud prevention, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure payments).

12. Payout details (bank account)

If you request a payout of your balance, you provide your name, address, IBAN and BIC for this purpose. This data is used solely to process the payout, stored encrypted and never returned to your browser in plain text. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

13. Storage period and deletion

We store personal data for as long as your account exists or as long as necessary for the purposes above. You can delete your account yourself in the settings at any time; this removes your profile, gameplay and connection data. Payment and accounting data may also be kept longer in restricted form due to commercial and tax retention obligations (usually 6 to 10 years).

14. Data security

This website uses TLS encryption (HTTPS) for secure data transfer. Sensitive data such as bank details is additionally stored encrypted in the database.

We reserve the right to amend this privacy policy so that it always meets current legal requirements or to reflect changes to our services.
